# Judicial Document Samples

This directory contains sample judicial documents from the Superior Court of Justice of Brazil:

- **`202500001724_DECISAO.pdf`**: Sample of a monocratic decision (*decisão monocrática*).
- **`202500069962_ACORDAO.pdf`**: Sample of a collegiate court judgment (*acórdão*).

## Purpose

These files provide concrete examples of the raw judicial documents processed by the framework. They allow inspection and qualitative analysis of the textual structure (e.g., judicial report/*relatório*, legal grounds, and operative outcome) of Habeas Corpus proceedings that are submitted to the language models for decision prediction.

## Public Data Source & Verification

These documents are public records from the Superior Court of Justice (STJ). The original proceedings and their full procedural history can be consulted publicly on the official procedural search portal:

🔗 **[Procedural Search Portal](https://processo.stj.jus.br/processo/pesquisa/?aplicacao=processos.ea)**

To look up each proceeding on the portal, use its corresponding case registration number (*número de registro*):
- `202500001724`
- `202500069962`
